What is Thila Homam?

A Sacred Vedic Ritual for the Peace of Our Ancestors

Thila Homam is a powerful Vedic ritual performed for the peace of souls of ancestors and family members who died unnaturally. It is also a remedy for those suffering from Pitru Dosha in their horoscope.

Rameswaram is the main place for performing Thila Homam. We organise the entire Homam as per the devotee's requirement — from Swamy Darshan in Rameswaram to performing Visarjanam in Dhanushkodi, along with food and accommodation arrangements.

Know More

The Sacred Place

Why Rameswaram?

Rameswaram is one of the 12 Jyotirlingas in India and is regarded as the most powerful place for performing Thila Homam. Devotees from across the country come here to seek relief from Pitru Dosha by satisfying their ancestors.

  • Home of the Ramanathaswamy Temple, one of the holiest shrines
  • Sacred waters and theerthams for purification rituals
  • Dhanushkodi — the sacred point for Visarjanam
  • Centuries of tradition in ancestral rituals

The Homam can be performed on any day in Rameswaram, or on any Amavasya.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Thila Homam?

Thila Homam is a powerful Vedic ritual performed for the peace of souls of ancestors and family members who died unnaturally. It is also a remedy for those suffering from Pitru Dosha in their horoscope. Rameswaram is the main place for performing Thila Homam.

Who can perform Thila Homam?

Thila Homam can be performed by anyone seeking peace for their ancestors or relief from Pitru Dosha. Families are welcome to participate in the ritual.

How long does the ritual take?

It takes around 3 hours to perform Thila Homam, depending on the count of chants chosen.

What should we bring?

Men should wear a dhoti and women should wear a saree or salwar for the ritual. The major ingredients of the Homam, such as sesame seeds (Til or Ellu), are arranged by the priests.

Is accommodation available?

Yes, accommodation and travel arrangements can be taken care of as part of the service.

Is food provided?

Yes, pure vegetarian food will be provided for devotees and families.

Can travel arrangements be made?

Yes, travel arrangements can be taken care of along with accommodation for devotees visiting Rameswaram.

Can families participate?

Yes, families are welcome to participate. Special darshan arrangements are made for devotees and families.

How can I book a date?

You can book a date by enquiring on WhatsApp or calling +91 87546 59663. The Homam can be performed on any day in Rameswaram, or on any Amavasya.

Where is the ritual performed?

Thila Homam is performed in Rameswaram, which is one of the 12 Jyotirlingas in India and the most powerful place for the Homam. Visarjanam is arranged at Dhanushkodi.
